## itsupport.online

IT support and software development from Berlin.

This account is where we publish our own open source work and contribute back
to the projects we build on.

### What we do

- **ERPNext / Frappe** — custom apps and integrations.
- **Web and mobile applications** — built for the cases where off-the-shelf
  software does not fit the process.
- **Networks and servers** — installation, monitoring, backup.

### Our projects

**[erpnext_qonto_banking](https://github.com/itsupport-onl/erpnext_qonto_banking)**
— a Frappe app that brings [Qonto](https://qonto.com) transactions into ERPNext
for bank reconciliation, without CSV exports or manual uploads. It reads from
the Qonto API and stops there: it never books anything and cannot move money.
Runs on ERPNext v15 and v16. AGPL-3.0, free to use, commercial support
available.

### Contributing

Issues and pull requests are welcome on any of our repositories. Security
reports belong in the `SECURITY.md` of the repository in question, not in a
public issue.

### Need something built or adapted?

What we publish here is free to use and stays that way. If you need an
integration, a change that fits your process, or simply someone to run and
maintain it for you, that is what we do commercially —
[talk to us](https://itsupport.online/contact/).
